## 3. Caveat and uncertainty register

The caveat phrasing for emerging-tier indications must not be softened in DE.

| EN | DE |
|---|---|
| The evidence base is currently anchored to a single research group; independent replication is awaited. | Die Evidenzlage stützt sich derzeit auf eine einzelne Forschungsgruppe; eine unabhängige Replikation steht aus. |
| may improve | kann verbessern |
| associated with | assoziiert mit |
| preliminary evidence suggests | vorläufige Evidenz deutet darauf hin |
| significant improvement (statistical) | signifikante Verbesserung |
| sustained at follow-up | bei Nachbeobachtung anhaltend |
| benefits sustained | Vorteile bleiben bestehen |
| meta-analytic confirmation provides supporting evidence | metaanalytische Bestätigung liefert unterstützende Evidenz |
| anchored to a single research group | auf eine einzelne Forschungsgruppe gestützt |

**Forbidden register** (must not appear in DE or EN):
- "transformative" / "transformativ"
- "breakthrough" / "Durchbruch"
- "revolutionary" / "revolutionär"
- "the answer to X" / "die Lösung für"
- "miracle" / "Wunder"

## 6. Proper nouns kept in original

The following are kept untranslated:

- HOT-LoCO, HEROES (trial names)
- UHMS, FDA, NFPA, GDPR, HIPAA (acronyms)
- Medicare / Medicaid
- Cochrane
- Author surnames in citations
- DOI URLs
- Journal names (e.g. *Sci Reports*, *BMJ Open*, *Frontiers in Medicine*)

## 7. Open items for reviewer

- **HBO vs HBOT**: lock on one or accept both. German clinical convention favours HBO; international SEO favours HBOT.
- **Tender Points vs Druckschmerzpunkte**: lock on one for fibromyalgia content.
- **Acne inversa vs Hidradenitis suppurativa**: both used in DE; reviewer to pick the going-forward form.
- **Emerging Evidence**: "Aufkommende Evidenz" vs "Sich entwickelnde Evidenz" — reviewer to pick.
- **First-use expansion convention** for RCT: align with the Greek convention (expand on first occurrence in any document; bare RCT thereafter).
